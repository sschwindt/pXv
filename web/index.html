Set video scale and location

A matching DJI SRT supplies camera height, gimbal angles, focal length and GPS. These let pXv convert pixel motion to metres per second and locate it on the survey map.

Without an SRT, use a known scale for metric speeds in a flat, downward-looking view, enter ground control points to match a georeferenced survey, or analyze in pixels per second.

Enter one water-plane point per line as image x, image y, map x, map y. Three points fit an affine map. Use four or more well-spread points for perspective correction. Pixel coordinates start at the top left.

Choose a GeoPackage point layer, GeoJSON, CSV, TSV or pXv survey JSON. Use metres per second for velocity and metres for depth.
